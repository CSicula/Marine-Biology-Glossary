# Equations and Maths Resources





## Equations



### Oceanography:



* \[p]
* Fg = (G \* m1 \* m2) / d^2 ; Force of gravity = (Gravitational constant \* mass of obj. 1 \* mass of obj. 2) / distance between objects squared.
* KE = 1/2 MV^2 ; Kinetic energy = 1/2 \* Mass \* Velocity squared.
* P = ρgh ; Pressure = Density \* Gravity \* Depth
* ΔPE = mgΔh ; Change in gravitational potential energy = mass \* gravitational field strength \* change in height
* V^2 = 2gΔh ; Velocity squared = 2 \* acceleration due to gravity \* change in height.
